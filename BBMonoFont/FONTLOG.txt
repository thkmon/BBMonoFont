FONTLOG for BB Mono Font
========================

Version 1.100 - 2026-08-20
--------------------------

- Product version is 1.1.0 (OpenType font revision 1.100).
- Keep imported Hangul and CJK glyphs unhinted through ttfautohint fallback
  scaling so their proportions progress smoothly at small pixel sizes.
- Add Hangul Jamo composition rules after autohinting.  This prevents GSUB
  feature analysis from assigning Latin hinting styles to modern Hangul.
- Preserve TrueType hinting for IBM Plex Mono Latin glyphs.

Version 1.000 - 2026-08-19
--------------------------

Initial public build.

BB Mono I:
- IBM Plex Mono Regular/Bold is the Latin and coding-symbol base.
- IBM Plex Sans KR supplies all 11,172 modern Hangul syllables.
- Sarasa Mono K supplies Hangul Jamo, Hanja, fullwidth characters, and other
  selected CJK blocks not assigned to IBM Plex Sans KR.

BB Mono S:
- IBM Plex Mono Regular/Bold is the Latin and coding-symbol base.
- Sarasa Mono K supplies modern Hangul and the selected CJK blocks.

Shared modifications:
- Latin advance remains 600 units.
- Hangul, Hanja, and fullwidth advances are fixed at 1200 units; Unicode
  halfwidth forms remain 600 units.
- Imported coverage is limited to 32,904 Korean coding-font code points:
  modern Hangul, Jamo blocks, 20,992 basic unified ideographs, CJK punctuation,
  and halfwidth/fullwidth forms.
- Every imported outline is decomposed, independently scaled, and horizontally
  centered in its 1200-unit cell.
- IBM Plex Sans KR scale: X 1.1363636364, Y 1.1363636364.
- Sarasa Mono K scale: X 1.10, Y 1.08.
- A single donor-wide vertical offset aligns each source to the measured median
  center of Sarasa Mono K modern Hangul.
- 11,172 Hangul Jamo composition rules are added to the ccmp feature.
- TrueType autohinting is applied with Windows compatibility enabled.
- Family and PostScript names are changed to avoid upstream Reserved Font Names.

The exact upstream binaries are identified by SOURCES.sha256.
